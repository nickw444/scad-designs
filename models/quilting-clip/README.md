# Quilting clip

A 70 mm outside-diameter, C-shaped spring clip for holding folded quilt layers
or binding while hand quilting. It is 10 mm high and has a 3 mm-wide spring
band around the diameter. Its end faces are rounded, and the base and top
edges have a small 45-degree chamfer for a fabric-friendly finish.

Print it flat as oriented in `quilting-clip.scad`, without supports. PETG is
recommended for repeated flexing; use 4 or more perimeters and 100% infill.
The Customizer controls the diameter, band width, height, and opening gap.
