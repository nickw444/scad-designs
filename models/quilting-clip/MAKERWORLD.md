# MakerWorld listing

## Title

Quilt roll support band for domestic sewing machines — 50 mm, 70 mm & 90 mm

## Description

A simple, reusable C-shaped spring band for keeping a quilt rolled or coiled
while it is fed through a domestic sewing machine. It keeps the bulk of the
quilt compact and controlled, helping it pass through the machine throat when
you do not have a long-arm quilting machine. The flexible band springs open
over the rolled quilt, while the 10 mm height holds the coil without a bulky
clamp.

Each clip has rounded ends to avoid catching fabric and small 45-degree
chamfers on the top and bottom edges for a smooth, fabric-friendly finish. The
model is printed flat and needs no supports.

## Sizes and STL files

| Outside diameter | STL file |
| --- | --- |
| 50 mm | `quilting-clip-50mm.stl` |
| 70 mm | `quilting-clip.stl` |
| 90 mm | `quilting-clip-90mm.stl` |

All three versions share these dimensions:

- Flexible band width: 3 mm
- Height: 10 mm
- Opening between rounded tips: approximately 12 mm
- Top and bottom chamfers: 0.5 mm at 45 degrees

## Print settings

- **Orientation:** Print flat, as supplied.
- **Supports:** None.
- **Material:** PETG is recommended for repeat flexing. PLA is suitable for
  occasional use but is more likely to crack if opened widely.
- **Walls:** At least 4 perimeters.
- **Infill:** 100%.
- **Layer height:** 0.20 mm is a good default.

For a first print, gently flex the band before use and test it on a rolled scrap
of quilt sandwich. Do not force it over a roll substantially thicker than its
opening.

## Customisation

Open `quilting-clip.scad` in OpenSCAD to change the outside diameter, flexible
band width, height, opening, or chamfer size.
