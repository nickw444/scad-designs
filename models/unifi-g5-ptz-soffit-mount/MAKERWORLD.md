# MakerWorld listing

## Title

UniFi G5 PTZ soffit mount – compact, +15 mm and +30 mm

## Description

A two-piece soffit mount for the UniFi G5 PTZ. The ceiling flange mounts first, then the camera and carrier attach to it using the supplied UniFi screws.

The mount reuses the original cable grommet and has been fitted and tested with the camera.

| Carrier | Raw STL height | Above camera mounting face | Camera mounting face to ceiling |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Compact | 33.9 mm | 18.5 mm | 23.5 mm |
| +15 mm | 48.9 mm | 33.5 mm | 38.5 mm |
| +30 mm | 63.9 mm | 48.5 mm | 53.5 mm |

The +15 mm and +30 mm carriers lower the camera to help it clear a fascia board or another nearby obstruction.

The raw STL includes 15.4 mm which wraps around the camera. “Camera mounting face” means the flat face with the Ethernet socket and threaded retention hole. The final column includes the 5 mm ceiling flange and gives the assembled gap between that camera face and the underside of the soffit.

## Files

- `unifi-g5-ptz-ceiling-flange.stl` — common ceiling flange for all three carriers.
- `unifi-g5-ptz-carrier.stl` — compact carrier.
- `unifi-g5-ptz-carrier-plus15mm.stl` — lowers the camera by 15 mm.
- `unifi-g5-ptz-carrier-plus30mm.stl` — lowers the camera by 30 mm.

## Print profiles

- ASA with ASA supports.
- PETG with PETG supports.
- PETG with a PLA support interface.

The profiles include the required supports. Remove them carefully and check that the parts fit together freely before assembly.

## Fitting

Insert the camera into the carrier and turn it into its seated position. Use one of the supplied UniFi screws through the internal shelf to secure the carrier to the threaded hole in the camera body.

Screw the flange to the soffit, push the assembled camera and carrier into the flange, then turn it clockwise as viewed from below until it stops. Use the other supplied UniFi screw through the rear hole in the carrier to secure it to the flange. This screw self-taps into the printed pilot hole when first installed.

The flange requires three suitable countersunk screws and fixings chosen for the soffit material.
