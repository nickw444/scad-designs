## Title

Sonos Era 100 flush ceiling mount

## Description

I designed this recessed mount so I could install a Sonos Era 100 in the ceiling with a slim, unobtrusive finish. The shell encloses the speaker to limit air exchange between the conditioned room and the open ceiling space; the power cable exits through a small rear opening with a TPU grommet. The speaker inserts from the room side and is held by two removable end retainers. A round grille clips over the ceiling flange. This is my own design, made specifically for the Era 100.

### Parts in the print profile

- **Plate 1:** Ceiling flange (`era-100-ceiling-flange.stl`) and two end retainers (`era-100-end-retainer.stl`), in PETG.
- **Plate 2:** Housing shell (`era-100-housing-shell.stl`), in PETG.
- **Plate 3:** Flat-cable split grommet (`era-100-flat-cable-grommet.stl`), in TPU.
- **Plate 4:** Large-hex grille (`era-100-hex-grille.stl`), in PETG.
- **Plate 5:** Fine-hex grille (`era-100-fine-hex-grille.stl`), in PETG.
- **Plate 6:** Plain grille for wrapping externally with speaker fabric (`era-100-grille.stl`), in PETG; this option has not been tested.
- **Plate 7:** Optional ceiling cutout template (`era-100-ceiling-cutout-template.stl`).
- **Plate 8:** Round-cable split grommet (`era-100-round-cable-grommet.stl`), in TPU.

Print one grille and the grommet that fits your lead.

The hex grilles can be used as printed. For less light and visibility through the openings, sandwich lightweight black fabric, such as thin interfacing, behind the grille during assembly. Keep it clear of the clips.

### Materials and assembly

- PETG for the rigid parts; TPU for the split cable grommet.
- **8 × M3 heat-set inserts (recommended):** approximately Ø4.4 mm body, Ø3.9 mm lead-in, fitting the printed Ø4.0 mm pilots (9 mm deep).
- **8 × M3 × 10 mm socket cap bolts:** 10 mm measured under the head, with heads no larger than Ø5.5 × 3 mm. Four join the flange to the shell; four secure the two retainers. The printed head pockets are Ø5.8 × 4 mm. Do not substitute 20 mm bolts.
- **6 × suitable 6g ceiling screws and fixings** for the ceiling material.

Heat-set the inserts into the shell. Pass the factory plug through the rear opening, then fit the split TPU grommet around the lead. Bolt the shell and flange together, fix the flange to the ceiling, insert the speaker from below, and bolt on both retainers. Finally, press the lined grille over the flange until its clips engage. Check your speaker, plug, ceiling fixing, and printed fit before installation.

This profile is designed for heat-set inserts. If you prefer self-tapping bolts, ask me for a separate insert-free version.
