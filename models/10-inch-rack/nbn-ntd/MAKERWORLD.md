# MakerWorld listing

## Title

NBN NTD 10-inch rack mount — 0, 1, 4 or 6 keystones

## Description

A family of 1⅓U open cradles for mounting an Australian NBN Network Termination
Device in a standard 10-inch rack. The NTD sits on the right in every variant,
with zero, one, four or six unnumbered keystone ports on the left.

## Variants and files

- No keystones: `nbn-ntd-no-keystone.stl`.
- One keystone: `nbn-ntd-1-keystone.stl`.
- Four keystones: `nbn-ntd-4-keystone.stl`.
- Six keystones: `nbn-ntd-6-keystone.stl`.

Each variant is a single printable mesh. PETG is recommended for rack use.

## Metadata

- **Category:** Server Rack Accessories
- **Tags:** `10 inch rack`, `nbn`, `ntd`, `keystone`, `network rack`, `australia`

## Disclaimer

Check the dimensions against your NBN enclosure before printing; NTD hardware
can vary between installations.
