# MakerWorld listing

## Title

Parametric sous-vide pot lid cover

## Description

A printable circular pot cover with a locating lip and a U-shaped opening for
a sous-vide circulator. The OpenSCAD source exposes the pot diameter, lip, lid
thickness and circulator cutout dimensions for easy adjustment.

## Files

- `sous-vide-pot-lid.stl` — default lid for a 241 mm outside pot diameter.

## Printing

Print flat in a temperature-resistant material appropriate for use near hot
water. Confirm the pot and circulator dimensions before printing.

## Metadata

- **Category:** Kitchen & Dining
- **Tags:** `sous vide`, `pot lid`, `kitchen`, `parametric`, `openscad`

## Disclaimer

Keep printed plastic clear of heating elements and do not treat it as a sealed
or pressure-rated lid.
