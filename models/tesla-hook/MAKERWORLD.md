# MakerWorld listing

## Title

Tesla trunk hook with captured M6 nut

## Description

A reinforced three-arm trunk hook for the exposed M6 bolt found in compatible
Tesla trunks. A side-loaded metal M6 nut replaces fragile printed threads, and
the loose bolt passage accommodates minor alignment variation.

## Hardware

- One standard M6×1 hex nut, 10 mm across flats.

## Files

- `tesla-hook-m6-nut.stl` — complete printable hook.
- `tesla-hook-m6-nut.3mf` — prepared print project.

## Printing and fitting

Print upright on the flat base. Slide the nut into the upper side opening,
place the hook over the exposed bolt, and rotate the hook to tighten it.
