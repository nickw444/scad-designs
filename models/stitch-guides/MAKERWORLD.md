## Title

Four sewing stitch guides with metric and imperial labels

## Description

A set of four flat sewing guides with their metric and imperial dimensions
debossed into the top face: bow, tails, knot and a 3½ × 3 inch rectangle.

## Files

- `stitch-guide-bow.stl` — 3½ × 7 in / 88.90 × 177.80 mm.
- `stitch-guide-tails.stl` — 3½ × 5 in / 88.90 × 127.00 mm.
- `stitch-guide-knot.stl` — 1¾ × 2½ in / 44.45 × 63.50 mm.
- `stitch-guide-rectangle-3.5x3.stl` — 3½ × 3 in / 88.90 × 76.20 mm.

## Printing

Print flat with the labels facing upward. No supports are required.
